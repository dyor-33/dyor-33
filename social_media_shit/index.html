Geo Pipeline

A private publishing tool for one educational YouTube channel.

Geo Pipeline is a personal, non-public tool. It researches public documents (patents, government records, and published studies), assembles educational videos from them, and uploads those videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

It is used only by the channel owner. It does not offer accounts, logins, or services to anyone else, and it does not access any other person's YouTube data.
